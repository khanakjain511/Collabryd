# Additional campus photograph prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset: supporting photographic image for Collabyrd's editorial landing and account panel.

Create a candid editorial photograph of three adult Indian university students, aged 20–24, working on a small software project together at a wooden table in an ordinary campus library. One woman is explaining a rough pen sketch on paper; a man and another woman are listening while looking between her notes and an unbranded laptop. Their attention is on each other, not on the camera. Capture an unposed moment from slightly above and to one side, like a photographer quietly documenting a real work session. Include natural personal details: a worn notebook, plain water bottle, loosely rolled sleeve, a pen left on the table. Keep the composition intentional and uncluttered.

Landscape 3:2, medium-wide, faces and shared work contained in the central area so the photograph also supports a portrait crop. Soft late-afternoon window light, gentle falloff, tactile wood and paper, realistic varied skin texture, ordinary cotton clothing in burgundy, cream and charcoal, subtle film grain. Comfortable campus setting with shelves out of focus. Warm, understated editorial color; no stylized glow, no heavy vignette, no forced smiles, no handshakes, no futuristic room, no extra fingers, no legible text, no logos, no watermark.

This is illustrative imagery, never presented as a photograph of actual Collabyrd members.
